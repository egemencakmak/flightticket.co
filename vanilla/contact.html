Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a flight? ▼

After searching for flights on Flightticket.co, you'll see results from our partner booking platforms. Click on any flight to be redirected to the booking site where you can complete your purchase securely.

Can I cancel or modify my booking? ▼

Since bookings are made directly with our partner platforms, you'll need to contact them for any changes or cancellations. Check your booking confirmation email for their contact information.

Why are prices different from what I saw initially? ▼

Flight prices change constantly based on availability and demand. The prices shown on our site are updated in real-time, but may change slightly when you click through to book.
